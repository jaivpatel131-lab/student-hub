About StudentHub

StudentHub is a student portal designed to help students manage their academic and campus activities from one platform.

Our Purpose

The purpose of StudentHub is to provide students with easy access to courses, events, academic information, profiles and feedback services.

StudentHub Features

Course Management

Students can view their enrolled courses and academic progress.

Events

Students can view upcoming college events and activities.

Student Profile

Students can view their personal and academic information.
